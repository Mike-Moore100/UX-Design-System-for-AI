# Microcopy

## What
Microcopy is the small text that helps users act, understand, recover, and feel confident in the interface.

Small copy often carries high user trust.

## Why it matters
Microcopy appears at moments of uncertainty.

Good microcopy:
- explains inputs
- reduces anxiety
- prevents errors
- clarifies next steps
- improves perceived quality

Microcopy should appear exactly where the user needs it.

## When to apply
- forms
- CTAs
- helper text
- empty states
- errors
- confirmations
- permissions and privacy moments

## How to apply

- answer the user's immediate question
- explain why sensitive information is needed
- keep helper text near the related control
- make errors actionable
- confirm success clearly
- remove microcopy that repeats obvious UI

Microcopy should reduce uncertainty, not add clutter.

## Implementation rules

- helper text must be close to the field or action
- microcopy must be specific to the moment
- sensitive requests need reassurance or explanation
- errors must explain how to recover
- success messages must confirm the result
- avoid decorative or redundant helper text

## Example

Bad:
- "Enter info"
- "Invalid"
- helper text explaining an obvious button
- privacy-sensitive fields with no explanation

Good:
- "Used only for billing receipts"
- "Password must include at least 12 characters"
- "Your invite was sent to maya@example.com"
- "You can change this later"

## Fail conditions

- user is unsure why information is needed
- error copy does not help recovery
- helper text is far from the control
- success state is vague
- microcopy adds noise without reducing uncertainty

## Enforcement rule

If microcopy does not answer a real user question, remove it or make it useful.
