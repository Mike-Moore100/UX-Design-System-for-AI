# Category Design

## What
Category Design frames the product inside a clear market category or creates a new category when existing labels fail.

The category should make the product easier to understand and compare.

## Why it matters
Users need a mental shelf to place a product on.

Strong category design:
- reduces explanation burden
- improves recall
- clarifies alternatives
- strengthens differentiation
- supports demand creation

Without a category, users may not know how to value the product.

## When to apply
- new products
- category-defining products
- repositioning work
- homepages
- launch pages
- investor or enterprise messaging
- comparison pages

## How to apply

- identify the existing category users already understand
- define why that category is insufficient if needed
- name the new or sharper category plainly
- connect the category to a painful market shift
- explain the stakes of staying with the old approach
- show why the product is built for the new category

The user should understand both what the product is and why the category matters.

## Implementation rules

- category language must be understandable
- new category claims must be justified by a real shift
- the product must visibly support the category promise
- alternatives must be framed fairly
- category copy must not replace concrete product explanation
- avoid inventing jargon for minor differentiation

## Example

Bad:
- coins a vague new category
- never explains the product
- attacks old tools without proving the shift
- uses terminology users would not repeat

Good:
- familiar category anchor
- clear reason the category is changing
- product capabilities match the category
- memorable but plain language

## Fail conditions

- user cannot tell what market the product belongs to
- category feels like invented jargon
- product does not substantiate the category claim
- message ignores real alternatives
- category language hides the value proposition

## Enforcement rule

If category language makes the product harder to understand, simplify the category before expanding the narrative.
