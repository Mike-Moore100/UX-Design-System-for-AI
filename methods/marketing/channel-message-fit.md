# Channel Message Fit

## What
Channel Message Fit adapts the message to the context, intent, and attention level of each acquisition or communication channel.

The same product promise may need different expression in different channels.

## Why it matters
Users arrive with different expectations depending on where they came from.

Good channel fit:
- improves relevance
- increases click quality
- reduces landing page mismatch
- supports better campaign learning
- lowers wasted attention

A message that works in one channel can fail in another.

## When to apply
- ads
- email
- organic search
- social posts
- referrals
- partner pages
- lifecycle campaigns
- product announcements

## How to apply

- define the user's likely intent in the channel
- match specificity to attention level
- keep the core promise consistent across source and destination
- adapt proof and CTA to the user's readiness
- measure quality beyond clicks
- avoid copying homepage copy into every channel

The message should feel native to the moment without changing the truth of the offer.

## Implementation rules

- channel copy must match user intent and awareness
- landing page must continue the same promise
- CTA must fit the channel's commitment level
- success metrics must include downstream quality
- channel constraints must not reduce clarity
- avoid clickbait that the product cannot satisfy

## Example

Bad:
- search ad promises a guide, landing page pushes demo
- cold email uses vague brand slogan
- social post overpromises for engagement
- same CTA across all awareness stages

Good:
- search message answers the query
- email opens with a specific pain
- social copy earns attention with a useful idea
- landing page continues the promise

## Fail conditions

- message feels out of place for the channel
- source and destination mismatch
- clicks increase but qualified conversions fall
- CTA asks for too much too soon
- channel-specific expectations are ignored

## Enforcement rule

If users arrive from a channel with a distinct intent, adapt the message and CTA to that intent.
