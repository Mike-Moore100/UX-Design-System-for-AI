# Local SEO

## What
Local SEO helps location-based businesses and services appear for searches tied to geography, proximity, and local intent.

Local pages must prove relevance to a real place and service area.

## Why it matters
Users searching locally often have high commercial intent.

Strong local SEO:
- improves map and local pack visibility
- builds trust
- clarifies service areas
- supports calls, bookings, and visits
- reduces irrelevant traffic

Generic pages rarely satisfy local intent.

## When to apply
- local service businesses
- clinics
- restaurants
- venues
- stores
- agencies with location pages
- regional landing pages

## How to apply

- state location, service, and availability clearly
- keep name, address, and phone information consistent
- add local proof such as reviews, projects, or landmarks when relevant
- create useful location pages instead of thin duplicates
- include opening hours, service areas, and contact actions
- connect pages to map listings and structured data where appropriate

The page should feel genuinely useful to someone in that location.

## Implementation rules

- location pages must contain unique local value
- NAP details must be accurate and consistent
- local CTAs must support calls, directions, bookings, or enquiries
- reviews or proof must be location-relevant when used
- schema must match visible local business data
- avoid doorway pages with swapped city names only

## Example

Bad:
- same page duplicated for every city
- address differs across site and listing
- no hours or contact clarity
- local page has no local proof

Good:
- clear service and area
- consistent contact details
- local testimonials or examples
- map, hours, and booking path visible

## Fail conditions

- page does not prove local relevance
- business details are inconsistent
- location content is thin
- CTA does not match local intent
- schema or listings are inaccurate

## Enforcement rule

If a page targets local intent, it must provide real location-specific value and accurate business details.
