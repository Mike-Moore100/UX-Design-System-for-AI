# Segmentation Personalisation

## What
Segmentation Personalisation adapts messages, offers, or flows to meaningful differences in audience, intent, source, or lifecycle stage.

Personalisation should increase relevance without feeling invasive.

## Why it matters
Different users convert for different reasons.

Good segmentation:
- improves relevance
- reduces generic messaging
- supports better CTAs
- increases qualified conversion
- clarifies proof and objections

One-size-fits-all pages often serve no one well.

## When to apply
- landing pages
- onboarding
- pricing pages
- email capture
- lifecycle campaigns
- product-led growth flows
- enterprise versus self-serve paths

## How to apply

- segment by meaningful intent or need
- adapt proof, examples, objections, and CTA by segment
- avoid unnecessary personal data collection
- let users self-select when identity is uncertain
- keep the underlying offer consistent
- monitor whether personalisation improves quality, not only clicks

The user should see a version that feels more relevant, not strangely tracked.

## Implementation rules

- segments must be actionable and distinct
- personalised content must match known or selected intent
- user control must exist where assumptions may be wrong
- sensitive personalisation must be avoided unless explicitly appropriate
- metrics must include lead quality or downstream activation
- fallback experience must still be strong

## Example

Bad:
- changes page copy based on weak assumptions
- references personal details unnecessarily
- creates many segments with no clear difference
- optimises clicks but lowers qualified leads

Good:
- self-select path by role or goal
- segment-specific proof
- CTA matched to readiness
- respectful fallback for unknown users

## Fail conditions

- personalisation feels invasive
- segment logic is unclear
- variants are not meaningfully different
- wrong segment harms comprehension
- conversion quality declines

## Enforcement rule

If personalisation does not make the experience more relevant and trustworthy, use a strong general experience instead.
