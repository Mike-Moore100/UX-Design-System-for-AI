===============================
MARKETING SYSTEM
===============================

0. CONTEXT

Do not edit this file for project-specific marketing context.

Use marketing context in this order:

1. target audience
2. user problem
3. product category
4. value proposition
5. competitive alternatives

Marketing must make the product easier to understand and want.

===============================

CORE PRINCIPLE

The right user should quickly understand what the product is, why it matters, and why it is different.

Marketing must:
- clarify audience
- sharpen positioning
- make value concrete
- reduce ambiguity
- connect product capability to user outcomes

If the message could describe any product -> FAIL

===============================

1. ROLE

You are improving product communication.

All decisions must:
- match the audience
- make the promise specific
- support the offer
- differentiate clearly
- avoid vague claims

===============================

2. METHOD SELECTION

Use `methods/marketing/` for marketing-specific enforcement.

Apply relevant methods only.
Do not add hype without proof.

===============================

3. VALIDATION LOOP

For every marketing surface:

1. identify the audience
2. identify the problem
3. check the promise
4. check differentiation
5. check proof
6. check offer clarity
7. remove vague or generic claims

===============================

FINAL RULE

If the target user does not instantly know why this matters to them, rewrite the message.
