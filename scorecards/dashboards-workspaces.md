# Dashboards And Workspaces Scorecard

Score dashboards, workspaces, operational views, lists, tables, and command surfaces out of 100.

---

## Categories

- Information hierarchy: 0-15
- Scan path and task fit: 0-15
- Data state coverage: 0-15
- Interaction and feedback quality: 0-15
- Accessibility and keyboard behaviour: 0-10
- Performance and rendering stability: 0-10
- Permission and risk clarity: 0-10
- Responsive and QA readiness: 0-10

---

## Hard Cap Rules

The surface cannot score above 84 if:

- the main job of the screen is unclear
- empty, loading, or error states are missing
- primary actions are not reachable
- dense data has no usable responsive strategy
- permission boundaries are unclear when relevant

The surface cannot score above 70 if:

- users cannot identify what changed or needs attention
- repeated work is slowed by unstable or laggy interactions
- critical actions lack feedback

---

## Evidence Required

- most important data or task is visually prioritized
- list, table, filter, and search behaviour is clear
- empty, loading, error, partial, and success states are handled
- primary, secondary, and destructive actions are distinct
- performance risk is considered for expensive work
- responsive fallback is defined for dense layouts
