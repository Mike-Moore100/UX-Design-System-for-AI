# Public Pages And Docs Scorecard

Score public pages, docs, blogs, product pages, comparison pages, landing pages, and discoverable content out of 100.

---

## Categories

- Intent alignment: 0-15
- Semantic structure and crawlability: 0-15
- Content usefulness and evidence: 0-15
- Metadata and internal links: 0-15
- Accessibility and readability: 0-10
- Performance and asset discipline: 0-10
- CTA or next-step fit: 0-10
- Freshness and QA readiness: 0-10

---

## Hard Cap Rules

The page cannot score above 84 if:

- title, H1, and opening content do not align
- important content is not crawlable
- metadata is missing for a public page
- claims lack support
- semantic structure is broken

The page cannot score above 70 if:

- users cannot tell what the page is for
- content is too vague to answer the target intent
- accessibility or performance issues block useful reading

---

## Evidence Required

- page intent is clear within the title, H1, and first section
- crawlable content answers the main user questions
- headings create a useful outline
- metadata and internal links support discovery
- claims have proof where trust matters
- performance and accessibility risks are checked
