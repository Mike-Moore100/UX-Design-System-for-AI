# Pricing Scorecard

Score pricing pages, plan comparison, upgrade entry points, and sales qualification paths out of 100.

---

## Categories

- Plan clarity: 0-15
- Primary action strength: 0-15
- Comparison quality: 0-15
- Trust and proof: 0-15
- Risk reversal and objection handling: 0-10
- Accessibility and mobile usability: 0-10
- Pricing copy clarity: 0-10
- Measurement and QA readiness: 0-10

---

## Hard Cap Rules

The page cannot score above 84 if:

- no clear recommended path exists
- pricing or billing terms are unclear
- primary CTA is not visually dominant
- plan comparison fails on mobile
- proof does not support the main claim
- payment or upgrade risk is not explained when relevant

The page cannot score above 70 if:

- users cannot determine which plan fits them
- CTAs do not explain what happens next
- critical pricing information is hidden

---

## Evidence Required

- recommended plan or best-fit path is visible
- each plan has audience and value clarity
- pricing terms are near the decision point
- proof, FAQ, and risk reversal address real hesitation
- mobile comparison remains usable
- funnel measurement is defined when analytics are in scope
