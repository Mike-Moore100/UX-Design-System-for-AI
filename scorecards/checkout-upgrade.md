# Checkout And Upgrade Scorecard

Score checkout, payment, subscription change, upgrade, downgrade, renewal, cancellation, and paid confirmation flows out of 100.

---

## Categories

- Cost and terms clarity: 0-15
- Confirmation and action safety: 0-15
- Payment or plan-change recovery: 0-15
- Trust, security, and privacy: 0-15
- Flow friction and completion: 0-10
- Accessibility and form quality: 0-10
- Analytics and funnel readiness: 0-10
- QA and edge-state coverage: 0-10

---

## Hard Cap Rules

The flow cannot score above 84 if:

- price, billing period, trial, or renewal terms are unclear
- selected plan or item is not confirmed before action
- payment errors are not recoverable
- sensitive plan changes lack clear confirmation
- duplicate submissions are possible

The flow cannot score above 70 if:

- users cannot verify what they are buying or changing
- critical payment or account risk is hidden
- success or failure states leave users stuck

---

## Evidence Required

- selected item, total cost, billing timing, and terms are visible
- confirmation copy explains impact
- payment and plan errors have recovery paths
- trust and security copy appears near high-trust actions
- duplicate submits are prevented
- key funnel and failure events are defined
