## Hard Gates

- one recommended path is visually clear
- each plan explains who it is for
- price, limits, billing period, and trial terms are visible before action
- primary CTA is dominant and specific
- proof supports the main claim
- risk reversal appears close to the primary action
- enterprise or sales paths do not compete with self-serve conversion
- plan comparison works on mobile
- upgrade or payment risk is explained when relevant

---

## Production Rules

- Reduce plan comparison to the few differences that affect the decision.
- Make the default plan obvious and explain why it is recommended.
- Keep secondary actions visually quieter than the primary action.
- Put billing terms and limits near price, not hidden in FAQ copy.
- Place proof near the claim it supports.
- Use FAQ content to remove real objections, not to store sales copy.
- Track pricing view, billing toggle, plan select, trial start, contact sales, and FAQ expand when analytics are in scope.

---

## Common Failure Patterns

- all plans feel equally important
- users must read every feature to decide
- billing terms are unclear
- proof is generic or far from the claim
- CTA copy hides what happens next
- mobile comparison requires excessive scanning
- enterprise path steals attention from the main self-serve path

---

## Evidence Required Before Scoring

- selected plan or recommended plan is identifiable within seconds
- price and billing terms are visible in the decision area
- primary CTA copy describes the next step
- comparison remains readable on mobile
- proof, risk reversal, and FAQ content reduce specific hesitation
- analytics or measurement plan covers the key conversion path
